06 · My SEO Process

My SEO Process.

I approach SEO as an ongoing system rather than a one-off checklist. Each stage connects research, implementation, measurement, and refinement.

  1. 01

    Research

    Understand the market, search intent, competitors, keyword opportunities, site structure, and business priorities.

  2. 02

    Audit

    Review Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, content quality, internal linking, Local SEO signals, and indexation performance.

  3. 03

    Strategy

    Prioritize actions based on impact, search demand, site condition, competitive gaps, and practical implementation requirements.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    Execute Technical SEO fixes, content improvements, metadata, internal linking, schema, Local SEO, and landing-page work.

  5. 05

    Measurement

    Track clicks, impressions, rankings, landing-page visibility, engagement, and conversion signals through relevant SEO and analytics tools.

  6. 06

    Iteration

    Use performance data to refine priorities, expand strong pages, resolve new issues, and keep the strategy aligned with search changes.
